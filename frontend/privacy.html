Privacy Policy

What Hiriq collects, why, who sees it and how you stay in control.

Last updated 12 November 2026

On this page
1. Who we are 2. What we collect 3. How we use it 4. Who sees it, and who we share it with 5. Cookies and browser storage 6. How long we keep it 7. Your choices and rights 8. How we protect it 9. Children 10. Changes 11. Grievance Officer
In short

We collect what we need to run your account and the features you use. We keep the text of your resume, not the file. We do not sell your data or use it for ads or to train AI. Recruiters see you only if you allow it or apply. You can edit, hide or delete your data and ask us to delete your account.

1. Who we are

Hiriq is operated by Zahid Shaikh, Mumbai, India. For the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000, we decide why and how the personal data described here is processed. Contact: hiriqoff@gmail.com.

If you are a candidate whose resume a recruiter uploaded, the recruiter decides why that resume is used and Hiriq processes it on their behalf. Please also read the recruiter’s own notice.

2. What we collect

DataWhy we need it
Account: name, email, password, date of birth, optional phone, designation and companyTo create and secure your account, verify your email and make your username. The password is stored only as a salted Argon2 hash. Date of birth is used to create the username and is never used in a score.
Google sign-in: your name and email from GoogleTo sign you in or create your account without a password.
Candidate data: the text extracted from your resume, the profile read from it (skills, roles, dates, education), your visibility choice, scores, tailored resumes, cover letters, interview prep and applications you sendTo run the features you use and to show your results back to you.
Recruiter data: jobs, uploaded resumes, edits to candidate profiles, pipeline stages, and notes and thumbs up or down on candidatesTo run your hiring workflow.
Security and service records: IP address and action in an audit log, and a log of each AI call (feature, model, success, speed and token counts, not the text)To keep accounts safe, investigate misuse and monitor how the AI features perform.

We keep the text we extract from a resume and a fingerprint (hash) of the file so the same file is not read twice. We do not keep the original file.

3. How we use it

  • To provide Hiriq: read resumes, score and explain matches, and run the candidate and recruiter tools.
  • To send emails you need, such as verification and password-reset codes.
  • To protect the service: prevent abuse, enforce limits and lock accounts after repeated failed sign-ins.
  • To improve reliability, for example by watching AI success rates and speed.

We do not sell your data, we do not show ads and we do not use your resume to train AI models. Name, gender, photo, college and date of birth are not inputs to any score.

4. Who sees it, and who we share it with

Other Hiriq users. A recruiter can see you in their candidate pool only if you turn on visibility. If you apply to a job, that job’s recruiter sees the resume copy, letter and message you chose to send, and your contact details.

Service providers (processors). We use these to run Hiriq:

  • Database hosting: Neon (PostgreSQL) stores account and application data.
  • AI models: OpenRouter and the model providers behind it receive the resume, job or question text needed for an AI feature. Questions you ask the pool are sent with resume excerpts but are not stored by Hiriq. Their own terms apply to what they receive. Please avoid putting information in a resume that you do not want processed this way.
  • Google: provides sign-in when you choose it.
  • Email delivery: an SMTP email provider delivers verification codes.
  • Job search feed: a third-party jobs API receives your search terms when you search outside jobs.
  • Hosting: the provider that serves the website and API.

Some of these providers process data outside India. We share only what each needs for its task. We may also disclose data when the law requires it or to protect people and the service.

5. Cookies and browser storage

Hiriq does not use advertising cookies or trackers. After you sign in, your session token is kept in your browser’s session storage and is removed when you sign out or close the tab. Google sign-in may set its own cookies on Google’s pages under Google’s policy.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Your resume and profile: until you delete them. Deleting your resume removes your profile, scores and the copies recruiters received through your applications.
  • Email codes and unfinished sign-ups: short-lived, and removed or expired within minutes to days.
  • Audit and AI-call records: kept for security and service history. They contain no resume text, and an audit entry keeps a label for the account so history stays accurate after deletion.
  • Recruiter uploads: until the recruiter deletes them or their account is deleted.

7. Your choices and rights

Under Indian law you have the right to ask for access to your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to withdraw consent, to get your questions and complaints answered, and to nominate someone to act for you if you die or cannot act.

  • In the app: edit your profile and what the resume reader got wrong, turn recruiter visibility on or off, withdraw an application, delete your resume and delete saved tailored resumes.
  • By email: write to hiriqoff@gmail.com to see your data, correct it, delete your account or withdraw consent. We reply within 30 days and may need to confirm that you are the account holder.

Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer provide features that need the data.

8. How we protect it

Passwords are hashed with Argon2. Sign-in attempts are limited and accounts lock for a while after repeated failures. Email codes expire quickly and allow only a few tries. Access to data is checked by role, and one recruiter cannot open another recruiter’s jobs or candidates. No online service is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Children

Hiriq is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has an account, write to us and we will remove it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as Hiriq changes. We will change the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app or by email.
