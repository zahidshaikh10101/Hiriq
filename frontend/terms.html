2. What Hiriq is

Hiriq is a web application that reads resumes, scores them against job descriptions and explains each score. Candidates can also tailor resumes, generate cover letters, prepare for interviews, search jobs and apply. Recruiters can write job descriptions, build a resume pool, rank candidates, ask questions of the pool and track a hiring pipeline.

Hiriq is not an employer, a recruitment agency or a job guarantee. We do not promise that you will get interviews, offers or hires. Jobs from outside sources come from third parties and we do not verify them.

The service is under active development. Features can change, move between plans or be withdrawn, and the service can be unavailable from time to time.

4. If you are a candidate

  • Your content is yours. You give Hiriq permission to store, read and process your resume and profile to provide the features you use, including sending text to AI models as described in the Privacy Policy.
  • Be truthful. Do not upload a resume that is not yours or that contains information you know to be false. Tailored resumes and cover letters are drafts: read and correct them before you send anything.
  • You control visibility. Recruiters can find you in their pool only if you turn on visibility, which is off by default. You can turn it off or delete your resume at any time.
  • Applying is your act. Nothing is sent to a recruiter until you press Apply. The recruiter receives a copy of what you chose to send.

5. If you are a recruiter

  • Only upload resumes you may use. You are responsible for having the right, and where required the consent, to upload and process each candidate’s resume. Use candidate data only for the hiring decision it was shared for.
  • Hire fairly. You must follow the laws that apply to your hiring, including those against discrimination. Hiriq never uses name, gender, photo, college or date of birth in a score, and Ask the pool refuses questions that filter people by gender, age, religion, caste, nationality, marital status or disability. Do not try to get around these protections.
  • A person decides. Hiriq ranks and explains. Scores are a decision aid, not a decision. You are responsible for every hiring decision you make.
  • Keep it confidential. Do not copy, scrape, sell or publish candidate information from Hiriq, and do not contact candidates in ways that break the law or their stated preferences.
  • Job posts must be real. Do not post fake, misleading or discriminatory jobs, or jobs that ask candidates to pay money.

6. AI features

Some features use AI language models: reading resumes, writing job descriptions, rewording resume lines, drafting letters and interview prep, a second look at skill gaps and answering questions about the pool. AI output can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. Hiriq limits this with checks, such as removing invented numbers and skills and verifying quotes against the resume, but cannot remove the risk. Review all AI output before you rely on it. See Responsible AI for how it works.
