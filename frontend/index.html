How it works

From resume to next step in five moves.

01

Upload

Drop in a PDF or DOCX. Hiriq reads skills, experience and education into fields you can correct. You choose whether recruiters can find you. It is off by default.

02

Understand

See how complete your profile is, which skills have evidence and which skills the open jobs ask for most.

03

Match

Browse open jobs, search outside sites or paste any job description. Each one gets a score with the reasons behind it.

04

Prepare

Tailor your resume to the job, generate a cover letter PDF and get interview prep. All of it comes only from what your resume backs up.

05

Apply

Send your resume or a tailored version with your letter and a message to the recruiter. Withdraw any time.

01

Post

Fill a short form and let AI write the job description. Edit it, then publish. Close or clone the job later.

02

Build the pool

Upload PDF or DOCX resumes. Opted-in Hiriq members and people who apply join the same pool.

03

Rank

Score everyone against the job with weights you set. Each score lists matched skills, gaps and the resume line that proves them.

04

Review

Ask the pool a question in plain English, read applicants' letters and resumes, and give a match thumbs up or down.

05

Move forward

Drag people along the pipeline from sourced to hired, with notes. You make every decision. Nothing is auto-rejected.
