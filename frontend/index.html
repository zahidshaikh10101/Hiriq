How it works

From resume to next step in three moves.

Upload

Drop in a resume. Hiriq pulls skills, experience and education into structured fields you can review and correct.

Match

Paste a job description or pick one from the library. Semantic matching produces a score with the reasons behind it.

Act

Close the gaps, apply to the best-fit jobs, or move the strongest candidates to the next stage.

Fairness

Built to be explained, not trusted blindly.

Scores show why

Every number comes with the matched skills, the missing ones and the experience gap.

Sensitive fields stay out

Name, gender, photo and college are kept out of the scoring features by design.

People decide

Hiriq ranks and explains. A recruiter makes every hiring decision. Nothing is auto-rejected.
