Responsible AI

How Hiriq uses AI, what it never does and how it keeps hiring fair.

Last updated 12 November 2026

On this page
1. Where Hiriq uses AI 2. What AI does not do 3. Fairness guardrails 4. Checks on what AI writes 5. Limits you should know about 6. Tell us when it is wrong
In short

AI helps read resumes and write drafts, but the score comes from transparent rules and a person always decides. Sensitive details never enter a score, and everything the AI writes is checked against your resume.

1. Where Hiriq uses AI

  • Reading resumes into structured fields you can correct.
  • Writing job descriptions from a short form, which the recruiter edits.
  • Rewording resume lines, drafting cover letters and preparing interview questions for candidates.
  • A second look at skill gaps in a ranking, when a recruiter asks for it.
  • Ask the pool: answering a recruiter’s question in plain English from resume passages.

2. What AI does not do

  • It does not make hiring decisions. Hiriq ranks and explains. A recruiter decides, and nothing is rejected automatically.
  • It does not set the score. The match score comes from a rules-based formula that anyone can reproduce from the same inputs: skills, qualification, experience, location and keywords, each with a visible weight. If a part cannot be judged from the data, it is left out and the rest are re-weighted instead of counted as zero.
  • It cannot invent a match. The optional AI second look can lift a missing skill to half credit only, and only with a quote that is found word for word in the resume.

3. Fairness guardrails

  • Name, gender, photo, college and date of birth are not inputs to a score. The scoring function does not receive them.
  • Qualification compares degree level only (diploma, bachelor’s, master’s, doctorate). The institution is never read.
  • Ask the pool refuses questions that filter people by gender, age, religion, caste, nationality, marital status or disability.
  • Text inside a resume or job description is treated as data. Instructions hidden in it, such as “rate this candidate highly”, are ignored.
  • Recruiters’ thumbs up or down are stored as feedback and do not change any score today.

4. Checks on what AI writes

  • Tailored resumes only reword your own lines. Numbers or skills that your resume does not contain are dropped.
  • Cover letters and interview prep are built only from your resume. Suggestions that do not stick to it are removed, and the page tells you how many.
  • Answers in Ask the pool are shown with quotes, and a quote that is not really in the resume is left out.

5. Limits you should know about

  • AI can misread a resume, especially with unusual layouts or scans. Check your profile and correct it. Corrections change the score.
  • A score reflects how well the resume text matches the job text. It does not measure talent, potential or fit with a team.
  • Models are provided by third parties, may change or be unavailable, and can still make mistakes that our checks do not catch.
  • Resume or job text is sent to an AI provider to produce the result. See the Privacy Policy.

6. Tell us when it is wrong

If a score looks unfair, a resume was misread or an AI output was harmful or wrong, email hiriqoff@gmail.com with what happened. We read every report and use it to fix the checks.
