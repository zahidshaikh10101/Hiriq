Refund & Cancellation Policy

How to cancel, when you can get your money back and how long it takes.

Last updated 12 November 2026

On this page
1. Plans and billing 2. Cancelling 3. Refunds 4. How to ask, and how long it takes 5. Price changes
In short

Cancel any time and keep access until the period you paid for ends. Get a full refund within 7 days of your first purchase. Yearly plans are one payment with 20% off. Mistaken charges are always refunded. Email us to ask and we reply within 2 working days.

1. Plans and billing

Hiriq has a free plan and paid plans for candidates (Basic, Pro, Premium) and recruiters (Pro and Enterprise). Prices are listed in Indian rupees on the pricing section. Applicable taxes, if any, are added at checkout.

  • Monthly plans are charged each month until you cancel.
  • Yearly plans are one payment for twelve months at 20% less than twelve monthly payments. They do not renew into monthly billing.
  • Enterprise is priced and agreed with you in writing. Its own agreement decides cancellation and refunds.

2. Cancelling

You can cancel a paid plan at any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for, and then moves to Free. We do not charge a cancellation fee. Cancelling does not delete your data. Delete your resume or ask us to delete your account if you want that.

3. Refunds

  • First 7 days. If you buy a paid plan for the first time, you can ask for a full refund within 7 days of paying.
  • After 7 days. Payments for a period that has started are not refunded, including the unused part of a monthly or yearly plan, unless the law requires it.
  • If we let you down. If we withdraw paid features you bought, or the service is unavailable for a long period, we will refund the unused part of your payment, calculated by month.
  • Mistaken or duplicate charges. We refund these in full.

4. How to ask, and how long it takes

Email hiriqoff@gmail.com from your account email with the subject “Refund” and the date and amount of the payment. We reply within 2 working days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method and usually reach you in 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank.

5. Price changes

If we change a price or what a plan includes, the change applies from your next billing period and we will tell you before it does. A price change never affects a period you have already paid for.
